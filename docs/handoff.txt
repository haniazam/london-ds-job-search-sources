Who this is for:
Hani Azam, a data scientist running a London job search for Data Science roles, who wants one exhaustive, self-updating list of live London DS postings with honest comp figures, kept as a Google Sheet, a job board artifact, and a GitHub repo of refresh tooling.

What we covered:
This session audited every link in the sheet, found 32 of 46 dead, and rebuilt it from employer job boards and APIs. It then widened the company universe from a fixed hand-curated list to a discovery sweep across 137 boards on nine ATS platforms, which surfaced 63 new live roles at 29 employers the old list had never checked. Every role's location was verified against the posting itself, estimated comp was replaced with a three-tier labelled scheme, a filterable board artifact was built, and the whole pipeline was turned into a reusable refresh skill in the repo. PR 3 was merged after resolving two conflicts toward main. The session ended with the request to move this work into the claude.ai job search Project, which is done by pasting this briefing there.

What was confirmed:
The Google Sheet (id 1tr7h-veYlLIQtewkBw8OY-Zz_7l-4cNNNjs8AO7BoSg, titled London DS Combined Active Job Posts) holds 78 verified-live roles at 39 companies in a nine column layout (Company, Role, Role Type, Level, Approx London TC, Location, Apply URL, Status, Notes), followed by a divider row and 19 no-role or unverified rows. The board artifact is https://claude.ai/artifact/UcMLHVF7Zfh5y67a77Skp3 at version 4. The repo haniazam/london-ds-job-search-sources has main at f2acc05, with later commits ab730f2 (discovery registry) and bc9cdad (three-tier comp) on branch claude/optimistic-ramanujan-80by3. The refresh-london-ds skill reads boards.json (137 boards across Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters, Pinpoint, Recruitee and Workday), runs refresh_roles.py, then pay_enrich.py with pay_overrides.json; SKILL.md documents the token harvest procedure. Scope is Standard DS titles only, London or London-eligible, both regular and senior, no intern or graduate roles, and recruiter boards are excluded. Comp coverage is 15 posted, 9 company-sourced, 11 per-company estimates and 43 market bands from ITJobsWatch London medians, every figure labelled. Only Google, Qube RT, Coinbase and GoCardless reach 130k GBP or more. A dead LinkedIn or Google post returns HTTP 200 with a generic page title, so liveness is judged by the title, never the status code. Google postings can close within a day of appearing.

Still in progress:
Seven leads are unverified rather than dropped: Checkout.com and IQVIA (SmartRecruiters API gated and the page renders blank even in a browser) and Warden AI, Uncapped, Day30, Clarity AI and Adzact (Workable rate-limited with 429).
